💡 UI/UX Audit: Resolving the "List View is also a Grid" Tablet Flaw
Material 3 Architectural Upgrade
⚠️ Previous Android Tablet Implementation
• Both Toggles Were Grids: ProductViewMode.LIST was hardcoded to LazyVerticalGrid(columns = GridCells.Adaptive(420.dp)), rendering wide 2-column cards rather than a true tabular list.
• Eye Fatigue: Eye path had to zigzag across columns; comparing prices, margins, and stock levels across rows was visually impossible.
• Wasted Screen Space: Large static valuation card (220dp) left only room for 4 product cards on a standard tablet screen before scrolling.
• Zero Batch Operations: Merchants couldn't bulk-select items to print shelf labels or inward purchase orders.
✓ Proposed Ergonomic Solution
• Grid View (Default in Tablet Mode): 3-column touch-friendly cards selected by default for fast visual POS cashier selling.
• Improved Streamlined List View: Sleek single-row stream showing the exact same product information as the cards (Title, Barcode, Category, Stock Pill, Selling Price, and 4 Action Buttons).
• No Data Mismatch: Removed cluttered ERP spreadsheet columns (purchase cost, margin %, checkboxes) to keep the catalog focused and consistent.
• High Vertical Density: Rapidly scroll through 12+ items with zero eye fatigue while keeping large touch targets.
• Collapsible Valuation Banner: 1-click collapse into a 38px ticker reclaims 160dp of vertical workspace.
