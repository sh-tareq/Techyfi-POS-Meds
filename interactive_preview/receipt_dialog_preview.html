💎 Photorealistic 3D Icons

Built with multi-layer radial gradients, specular highlights, and physics-inspired hover/float micro-animations matching the Techyfi POS design system.

🎯 Isolated Language Scope

The EN / বাং switch modifies only the thermal JPEG/PDF generation parameters without altering the app configuration or resetting the activity.

🇧🇩 Authentic Retail Phrasing

Guaranteed natural Bangladeshi terminology rather than robotic literal translations (e.g. সর্বমোট instead of গ্র্যান্ড মোট, ফেরত instead of পরিবর্তন).
