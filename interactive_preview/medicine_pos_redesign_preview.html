2. Twelve High-Value Pharmacy Improvements Proposed for Implementation

1. 💊 Three-Tier Unit Inventory Math

Store inventory internally in base units (loose tablets). When cashier sells 1 Strip of 10 tablets, automatically deduct 10 base units. Selling a box of 500 tablets deducts 500 units.

2. 🔄 Instant Generic Substitute Drawer

Indexed SQLite query matching generic_name = ? AND dosage_form = ? AND strength = ?, sorting results by lowest price and active stock availability.

3. 🚨 DGDA Antibiotic Rx Mandatory Verification

Visual amber/red alert trigger when scanning any antibiotic with is_antibiotic = 1. Captures Doctor's Name and BMDC license number in the invoice record.

4. 📋 DIMS Clinical Reference Sheet

Offline clinical pop-up displaying Indication, Adult Dose, Child Dose, Contraindications, Side Effects, and Pregnancy Warnings for all 1,425 generics.

5. ⏳ FEFO Expiry & Early Warning Reports

Automated daily morning alert highlighting medicines expiring within 30, 60, and 90 days so they can be returned to pharmaceutical distributors for credit.

6. 🏷️ Shelf / Rack Physical Location Navigator

Displays rack/shelf coordinates (e.g. Rack B-2) on POS search cards and cart preview so dispensers immediately know which cabinet to pull from.

7. 🧾 Sticky Medicine Packet Label Printing

Support for TSPL mini sticker printers (e.g. 50mm x 30mm) to print patient name, medicine name, and dosage instructions for sticking directly on pill packets.

8. 🛡️ Maximum Retail Price (MRP) Lock

Enforces Directorate General of Drug Administration (DGDA) price ceilings to prevent accidental over-charging above government-fixed MRP.

9. 🩺 Prescribing Doctor Registry & Sales Tracking

Directory of local clinic doctors to track which doctors refer prescriptions to this pharmacy, enabling pharmacy-clinic coordination.

10. ⚡ Pre-populated Room SQLite Database Migration

Ship techyfi_pos_meds.db directly in Android assets/ folder using Room's createFromAsset("techyfi_pos_meds.db") for zero-lag offline launch.

11. 📱 Repeat Prescription Patient Refill Reminders

For chronic disease patients (Hypertension, Diabetes, Asthma), track day supply (e.g. 30 days) and send SMS/WhatsApp reminders before they run out.

12. 🏢 Pharmaceutical Supplier Reconciliation

Pre-populated directory of 692 pharma companies (Square, Beximco, Incepta, Renata) with company representative contacts and purchase order logging.
