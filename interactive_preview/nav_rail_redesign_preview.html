🚨 Identified UX Flaws in Current UI:

Flaw 1 Icon Inconsistency & Optical Sizing: Products is an isometric box, Expenses is a squished 10px green bill, Settings is a glowing eye, and More is a lightning bolt. Optical weights vary by up to 240%.

Flaw 2 Active Capsule Color Clashing: Default M3 lavender pill (#E8DEF8) clashes with vibrant blue label (#0082FF) and multi-colored 3D icons, creating optical distraction.

Flaw 3 Tiny 9.5sp Text: Below recommended 11sp viewing threshold for POS counter distances (~60cm), causing cashier eye fatigue during busy rush hours.

Flaw 4 Excessive Width (84dp): Wastes 12dp of prime tablet landscape horizontal room while leaving awkward empty lateral padding.

Flaw 5 Missing POS Shift Quick Controls: No quick Cashier identity indicator or 1-tap Lock button. Cashier must navigate through Settings to lock the terminal.
