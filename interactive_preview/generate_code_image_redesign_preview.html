📊 Legacy Window vs. Redesigned Studio Comparison

❌ Legacy Window (Current Bulky Form)
  • • Endless Vertical Scroll: 10 separate cards stacked in a single monolithic column taking 92% screen height.
  • • Missing Product Context: Users couldn't see the product image, SKU, category, or real-time stock.
  • • No Product Carousel: Users had to exit the window and reopen for each separate item.
  • • Flat M3 Design: Lacked visual depth, 3D cues, or tactile interactive feedback.
  • • Static Small Preview: Fixed 70-130dp preview without zoom inspection or thermal dot simulation.
✅ Redesigned Studio (Compact & Ergonomic)
  • • Compact 3-Tab Architecture: Setup, Store, and Dates organized into 3 zero-scroll tabs.
  • • Product Hero Carousel: High-contrast card with ◀ / ▶ arrows to view and label any item sequentially.
  • • In-Dialog Product Picker: Instant product selection without closing or losing settings.
  • • All 6 Scale Sliders: Product Name, Price, Code Number, Store Name, Date Tracking, and Barcode Graphic.
  • • Realistic 3D Animated Badges: Ambient colored drop shadows, specular glints, and press transforms.
  • • High-Fidelity Zoom Modal: Simulated 203 DPI thermal dot density and scanner clarity inspection.
