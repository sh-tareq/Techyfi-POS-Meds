System Architecture Comparison

Clean Separation of Concerns: Drawer Cash vs Shop Expenses

❌ Previous Layout (Confused Responsibilities)

• The "Today's Drawer Cash" vault card was situated inside Expenses & Cash screen.
• Float In (`+ Cash In Float`) was mixed in with ordinary shop expenses.
• Staff going to close the day in Day Close had no visual connection with the cash float card they saw in Expenses.
• No Bangladeshi denomination note counter was present.

✅ New Redesigned Layout (Clear, Intuitive, Powerful)

• Day Start & Close now acts as the unified command center for all cash register operations (Opening Float, Mid-shift Float In, Safe Drop, Shift Timer, Live Drawer Cash, Discrepancy Detection, and Z-Report closing).
• Expenses is now a pure, dedicated expense ledger focusing 100% on expense analytics, category distribution, budget health, and receipts.
• Added Interactive Bangladeshi Note Denomination Counter (৳1000 - ৳1) with live tallying.
• Photorealistic 3D animated iconography (`3d_cash_vault`, `3d_day_shift_audit`, `3d_expense_analytics`, `3d_taka_stack`) delivers an ultra-premium look.
