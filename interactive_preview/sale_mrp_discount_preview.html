💡 Proposed UX & UI Enhancements for Confirmation

We analyzed modern retail workflows (Shwapno, Agora, Unimart) and designed these 4 enhancements to maximize customer delight and cashier speed.

Design System Ready
Feature 1 • Instant Delight

🎉 Real-time Header Savings Pill

Directly on the checkout screen header, cashiers immediately see the green pill badge: "Customer Saves ৳100 (20%) vs MRP". This empowers cashiers to verbally tell customers their exact savings before completing payment.

Feature 2 • Value Transparency

✨ Customer Savings Breakdown Card

An emerald-bordered card clearly separates MRP Total (৳500), Sale Subtotal (৳400), and Total Customer Savings (৳100). Automatically factors in any extra manual discount entered.

Feature 3 • Strict Mode Isolation

🛡️ Strict Sale Mode Activation Only

In MRP Mode, customer pays full MRP — all savings pills and banners are completely hidden. In Bulk Mode, wholesale discounts are shown instead. Never causes misleading discount claims.

Feature 4 • High-Impact Receipt

🧾 Celebratory Receipt Delight Banner

Prints an eye-catching banner: *** আপনি সাশ্রয় করেছেন: ৳১০০.০০ (২০%) *** right below the Grand Total on thermal slips, proven to increase repeat shopper loyalty.
