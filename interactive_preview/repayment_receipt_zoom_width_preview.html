💡 Additional Suggested Improvements

Professional UI/UX recommendations to elevate the merchant and customer experience:

⚡ 1. Direct Customer WhatsApp Dispatch

Since the customer's phone number (01919207715) is already captured in the voucher, a single tap can immediately open WhatsApp with the formatted voucher message preloaded—saving cashiers 15+ seconds per transaction!

📳 2. Tactile Haptic Confirmation Feedback

Trigger a subtle physical haptic vibration (HapticFeedbackType.LongPress or TextHandleMove) on Print and Copy actions, confirming operation success even in noisy retail environments.

⏱️ 3. Animated Reactive Print Button State

Upon tapping Print, the button shows a 1.2s "Printing..." spinner state with the 3D printer feeding paper, transitioning to "Printed ✓" before returning to default.

📏 4. Automatic Dialog Height Conservation

By trimming ~28dp to 36dp from the bottom deck, the thermal receipt chamber gains extra vertical space, ensuring the full repayment voucher (including barcode and signature) fits on screen without requiring scrolling!
